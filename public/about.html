[ 01 ] — What it does

Everything you need
for doctoral research

No API keys required. All 22 academic databases are searched simultaneously on every query — completely free.

01

Dialectic Engine

Seven supervision modes — Supervise, Critique, Sources, Consistency, Expand, Examiner, and Draft — each calibrated to a different stage of doctoral work.

AI Supervision

02

22 Databases in Parallel

BAILII, OSCOLA, Google Scholar, JSTOR, PhilPapers, arXiv, Semantic Scholar and 15 more — searched simultaneously on every query.

Research

03

Research Vault

Upload PDFs, case law, and notes. HoRatio searches your private library and cites from it automatically in every response.

Library

04

Reference Manager

Paste a bibliography or a single citation — HoRatio splits, categorises, and stores every reference with automatic OSCOLA formatting.

Citations

05

Viva Simulator

An AI external examiner conducts a full oral viva on your submitted work — rigorous, adversarial, and formative before the real thing.

Viva Prep

06

Slide Discussion

Upload PDF presentations and discuss them slide by slide. Navigate, annotate, and rehearse conference presentations with AI feedback.

Presentations

07

Living Journal

Auto-generated research journal that logs every session. Export supervisor-ready progress reports from your research history.

Journaling

08

OSCOLA Footnotes

List sources in footnote order — HoRatio generates properly formatted OSCOLA footnotes with ibid and above n shortening automatically.

Citations

09

Research Relay

Bridge your Gemini Gem or Claude Project into HoRatio — paste any response and citations are extracted into your library automatically.
